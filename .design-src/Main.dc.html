Built into the wireframes What makes Appointex different, booking a whole team in one checkout, carries the Home carousel and the event bundle screen. Keeping bookings and payments inside the app carries the screened chat, escrow style held payments, ID verified badges and loyalty pricing on the business side. Featured Spots is the paid advertising layer, category placement (up to 3 providers per category, shown at the top of that category's search results) and a Home banner ad rotating in the carousel every client sees, both clearly marked to clients as paid rather than earned, and both bought from the same screen on the business dashboard. Urgent booking is the same-day layer: a client picks a category and an urgency window, the app searches nearby providers of that category and surfaces only the ones with a real open slot in that window, and a transparent percentage rush fee (15–40%, set by Appointex, not the provider) is shown before checkout. Mobile service lets a client ask their provider to travel to them instead of visiting the salon, for a flat 5% travel fee on top of the service price, shown as a "Where would you like this?" choice on the booking screen and carried through to checkout. Both are opt-in per provider, toggled on their Settings screen.
